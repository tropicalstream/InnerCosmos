Crew voice clips (generated by ../../../tools/generate_fish_audio.py from
tour_script.json with Fish Audio S2-pro).

Layout the app looks for:
  voice/navigation/<clip>.ogg    the Pilot / Helm   (.ogg preferred, .wav also accepted)
  voice/science/<clip>.ogg       the Physiologist ("Doc")
  voice/engineering/<clip>.ogg   the Engineer

<clip> is the "clip" key of each cue or filler in tour_script.json.

If a clip is absent the app tries, in order: a Fish Audio clip cached from an
earlier run, live Fish Audio synthesis (key + voice ids from
app/tools/fish_audio.config, baked into BuildConfig), then on-device
TextToSpeech with a distinct per-role voice. So the tour works before any clips
are generated, but pre-rendered clips sound best and need no network.
